# Manual tests

Verifies inside Obsidian the behaviours of ekphrasis that unit tests cannot reach, such as focus, rendering and changes made by other applications

- [Test protocol: Bound form](protocols/bound-form.md): verifies the bound form inside Obsidian
- [Test protocol: Saving of scalars](protocols/saving.md): verifies the saving of scalar fields inside Obsidian
- [Test protocol: Validation](protocols/validation.md): verifies the validation of instances inside Obsidian
- [Test protocol: Corrections after the check of Validation](protocols/corrections-validation.md): verifies inside Obsidian the corrections made after the check of validation
- [Test protocol: Collections](protocols/collections.md): verifies the collection kinds inside Obsidian
- [Test protocol: Guidance and navigation](protocols/guidance-navigation.md): verifies the guidance and the navigation of the form inside Obsidian
- [Test protocol: Corrections after the check of Guidance and navigation](protocols/corrections-guidance.md): verifies inside Obsidian the corrections made after the check of guidance, together with the premises of the milestone Links
- [Test protocol: Links](protocols/links.md): verifies inside Obsidian the following, the suggestion and the copy of field links
- [Test protocol: Corrections after the check of Links](protocols/corrections-links.md): verifies inside Obsidian the corrections made after the check of links
- [Test protocol: Schema registry](protocols/schema-registry.md): verifies inside Obsidian the binding of records through the schema registry
- [Test protocol: Form presentation](protocols/form-presentation.md): verifies inside Obsidian the presentation of the rows of fields, of their controls and of the outline
- [Scenario records](scenarios): supplies the records and the schemas of the protocols, copied into the test vault at each build
- [Settings of the test vault](vault-settings.json): fixes the settings from which every protocol starts
